## About the Blog

VORQ Blog publishes personal and analytical articles about society, economy, politics, family, culture, technology, life topics, and ideas.

The platform is intended for reflection, discussion, and knowledge sharing. It is not an official news platform, government source, legal advisory service, financial advisory service, medical service, or professional consulting service.

Articles and opinions may reflect the views of their individual authors. Readers should verify important information from official or specialized sources before relying on it.

## Project Structure

- `index.html`: Main public landing page for visitors and published articles.
- `admin.html`: Private administrator page for managing writer invitations and system access.
- `hk-writers-access.html`: Private writers portal for login and access to publishing tools.
- `create-post.html`: Page for creating and publishing blog posts.
- `manage-posts.html`: Page for editing, filtering, or deleting posts according to user permissions.
- `employee-register.html`: Writer registration page through an invitation link.
- `employee-contract.html`: Writer responsibility agreement.
- `legal.html`: Legal information page for VORQ Blog / VORQ Group.
- `privacy.html`: Privacy policy describing data handling and user rights.
- `terms.html`: Terms of use for the blog platform.
- `disclaimer.html`: Disclaimer explaining the limits of responsibility for articles, opinions, links, and external sources.
- `404.html`: Not-found page.
- `vorq-group-logo.svg`: Main logo used in the blog header and legal pages.

## Technical Notes

The project uses Firebase services for authentication, database functions, hosting, and access control. Access to administrative and writer pages should always be protected through Firebase Authentication and database/security rules.

Do not expose private admin links unnecessarily on the public homepage. Hidden pages are not a complete security measure; real protection must come from authentication and database rules.
